# Disabled Turkish trading content

This directory preserves the Turkish trading/finance content that was deactivated on 2026-09-14.

- The original articles are preserved unchanged under `articles/`.
- The former trading mentoring and education pages are preserved unchanged under `pages/`.
- Pre-deactivation copies of the edited landing, about, and article-index pages are under `archive/`.

To restore the content, move the desired files back to their original paths and restore the corresponding navigation, page sections, article index entries, and sitemap URLs.
